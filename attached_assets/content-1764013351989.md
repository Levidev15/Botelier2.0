The Direct Booking API empowers hotel groups to unlock the full potential of our Direct Booking product. This powerful API delivers information on your properties, including rate plans, room types, room rates, and real-time rate and availability data across your entire property portfolio.

## [Direct link to heading](https://developer.siteminder.com/siteminder-apis/properties/introduction/direct-booking-api\#key-benefits)    Key Benefits

1. Streamline guest booking experience: Create consistent, branded guest booking journeys on customised websites to maximise the effectiveness of direct booking conversions

2. Access real-time data: Get up-to-the-minute information on availability and pricing to ensure data accuracy and consistency across all your properties. :

3. Secure and reliable integration: Enjoy peace of mind with secure data exchange and dependable performance around the clock. The API ensures your custom implementations work flawlessly, allowing you to focus on delivering exceptional guest experiences.


## [Direct link to heading](https://developer.siteminder.com/siteminder-apis/properties/introduction/direct-booking-api\#how-it-works)    How it Works

Our hoteliers leverage this robust API solution to create seamless connections between our Direct Booking system and their existing applications, websites, and marketing platforms.

With this API, you can create distinctive digital experiences for your guests, differentiate your property in the marketplace, and maximise the effectiveness of your direct booking channel—all while maintaining complete control over your brand presentation.\

1. **Generate your API key** in Multi-Property > Direct booking > API

2. **Make API calls** to retrieve:



1. Property listings and details

2. Room types and configurations

3. Rate plans and pricing

4. Real-time availability and quotes


3. **Use the data** to build custom interfaces such as:



1. Pricing and availability calendars

2. Custom booking workflows

3. Special offer landing pages


4. **Data syncs automatically** as changes are made in Direct Booking, ensuring your website always displays current information.
